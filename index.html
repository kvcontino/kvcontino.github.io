He cultivated learning and loved the ancients, seeking truth from facts.This is not Mao's phrase, although he made a slogan of it. It comes from the Book of Han, compiled by Ban Gu around 111 CE, in the biography of Liu De, Prince Xian of Hejian, who collected and collated texts that had been scattered in the wars ending the Qin. It described a method of study, not a political program. Primary text at the Chinese Text Project.
