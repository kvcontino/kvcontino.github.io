Medicaid Drug Payment Spread

Kevin V. Contino

This summary compares Medicaid reimbursements to what pharmacies pay for each drug. State comparisons are generated from State Drug Utilization Data State Drug Utilization Data (SDUD) is filed with CMS by every state Medicaid agency under the Medicaid Drug Rebate Program and published quarterly at data.medicaid.gov. Each row is a state × NDC × quarter: units reimbursed, prescriptions, and total amount reimbursed. This page uses the 2025 annual file with fee-for-service and managed-care utilization combined. Amounts are pre-rebate, so they measure what the pharmacy was paid, while federal and supplemental rebates settle separately and never enter the file. Suppression applies at eleven or fewer claims, which thins small states on small drugs. Roughly half the 2025 dollars are filed under the state code XX, which every state-level figure here excludes: CMS publishes national totals in the same file as the states, and XX is the code those rolled-up rows carry, so counting them alongside the states would count the same dollars twice. and Average Drug Acquisition Cost National Average Drug Acquisition Cost (NADAC) is CMS’s survey of what retail community pharmacies pay wholesalers, published weekly as a per-unit rate by NDC. It is a national benchmark. The survey covers retail pharmacies, so it prices a 340B or specialty-pharmacy channel only by approximation. Rates move through the year; this page uses each NDC’s last rate effective on or before 2025-12-31 as a single reference point..

Method

SDUD 2025 annual file (fee-for-service plus managed care utilization combined), aggregated to state × NDC, joined to the NADAC rate effective closest to and on or before 2025-12-31 for that NDC. Drug names are the FDA product name from the MDRP product-list file, because SDUD’s own name field truncates at about ten characters at the source. Package-size and dose-strength variants of the same brand are summed together. Spread = reimbursed dollars − (units × NADAC rate); percent spread is that gap over the acquisition-cost total.

  • Unit basis assumed, not row-verified. SDUD “Units Reimbursed” and NADAC “Pricing Unit” are both NDC-billing-unit based (each / mL / gram) and should align per NDC by construction. This was not checked row by row, and a mismatch would inflate or deflate that NDC’s spread.
  • Year-end NADAC snapshot. Rates move through the year; each NDC is priced at its last known 2025 rate rather than at the rate in force during the quarter it was billed in.
  • 91.3% of reimbursed units matched a NADAC rate by total volume. Unmatched NDCs (compounds, some brand-only products, discontinued drugs) are dropped from the analysis rather than estimated.
  • Half of matched SDUD dollars are national-total rows, not state rows. CMS publishes rolled-up national totals in the same file, coded XX: $83.1B of the $164.9B in NADAC-matched 2025 reimbursement. Those rows repeat utilization the states have already reported, which is why summing the match without excluding them comes out close to double. Every state-level figure and the national +7.1% headline drop them. Territories (PR) are shown; PS is also excluded.
  • A negative spread is not a finding about a state’s fee schedule on its own. The deepest cells here are single NDCs where reimbursement fell far under the national acquisition benchmark — a 340B-acquired product, a supplemental-rebate arrangement, or a data-entry artifact all produce the same shape in this file.
